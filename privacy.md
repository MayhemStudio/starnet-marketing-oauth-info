# Privacy Policy — StarNet Marketing Rclone

**Last updated: October 26, 2026**

## 1. Overview

StarNet Marketing Rclone is a private, owner-operated application that connects Google Drive to an AI-assisted marketing workflow using Rclone.

The application is intended for internal business operations and is not offered as a public service.

## 2. Information Accessed

With authorization from the Google account holder, the application may access:

- Google Drive files and folders.
- File names, identifiers, metadata, and permissions.
- Images, documents, and other authorized file contents.
- Information necessary to authenticate and maintain the Google Drive connection.

The application requests Google Drive permissions that technically allow reading, creating, modifying, and deleting files available to the authorized account.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Google Drive access is used to:

- Retrieve approved brand assets and marketing references.
- Organize authorized marketing materials.
- Upload completed creative deliverables.
- Verify file integrity and manage authorized project files.

The operator intends to limit file operations to designated marketing project directories.

## 4. Data Storage

Original creative assets and completed deliverables are stored in Google Drive.

OAuth credentials are stored locally in an owner-controlled Rclone configuration on the Ubuntu system.

Temporary processing may occur when files are accessed or transformed. The application is not intended to maintain a separate permanent synchronized asset library.

## 5. Information Sharing

Google Drive information is not sold to advertisers or data brokers.

The Rclone connection communicates with Google's APIs to perform authorized file operations.

When separately authorized marketing workflows use AI services, selected materials may be processed by those services according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Files remain in Google Drive until modified or deleted by an authorized account holder or operation.

The account holder may revoke access through Google Account security settings.

Locally stored authentication credentials can also be removed by the application operator.

## 7. Security

The operator uses restricted local configuration permissions and intends to limit automated file operations to authorized project directories.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Access permissions and operational controls are reviewed as the application develops.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning this private integration, contact the application operator using the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.

## 9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality, integrations, or data-handling practices.
